Privacy Policy — Encaixa

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Encaixa is a block puzzle game for Android. This page explains what data the game uses, what for, and how you control it.

1. Who is responsible

Responsible: Agster Junior Da Costa Santos
Contact: focodevsistemas@gmail.com

2. We do not ask for an account or personal data

You play without signing up, without logging in, and without giving your name, email or phone number. Your progress (levels, stars, gems, best score, themes and settings) is stored on your device.

3. Automatic progress backup and Google Play Games

So you do not lose your progress when you reinstall the game or switch phones, the game may keep a small copy of it in Google services: Block Store and Android backup (when turned on) and Google Play Games (“Saved games”, when you sign in with your account). These copies are handled by Google under Google's privacy policy and are tied to your Google account. We do not have access to them.

Global ranking: if you are connected to Google Play Games, the game sends your score to Google's leaderboard. The leaderboard may show your Google Play Games player name and your score to other players. You control this in the Google Play Games settings.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. For this, AdMob may collect and use the device's advertising ID, IP address, device information (model, system version, language) and ad usage data, to show ads (including personalized ones), measure results and prevent fraud.

  • Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game shows Google's consent form before loading ads.
  • You can turn off ad personalization at adssettings.google.com and reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Learn how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. In-game purchases

Purchases (gems, "No ads", the Encaixa+ subscription and items) are made and charged by Google Play. The game does not receive or store your card or payment details. We only keep on the device what was purchased, to unlock the item. You manage and cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store.

6. Sharing with friends

The share buttons use Android's share sheet and send only the text with your score, to the app you choose. We do not send anything on our own.

7. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a notification on the device itself. It does not send data to any server, and you can turn it off at any time in the game or in Android settings.

8. Children

Encaixa is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. How long we keep data and how to delete it

  • Data on the device: stays until you uninstall the game or clear the app data.
  • Google copy: to delete it, use your device's Google backup settings (Settings > Google > Backup).
  • Ad data: controlled by Google (see item 4).

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the LGPD in Brazil or the CCPA in California), you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data and withdraw your consent. Since the game does not store personal data on our servers, almost everything can be done by yourself as described above. For any request, use the contact in item 1.

11. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using other features (for example, measuring game usage with analytics tools), this page will be updated beforehand and the date at the top will change.
